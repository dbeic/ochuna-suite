What We Build

💰 Fintech — Harambee Cash App

A 30-second round game. KES 10 entry, random winner takes 90% of the pool. New users start with a KES 200 welcome loan, repaid from winnings at KES 10 per win. Only winnings are withdrawable; minimum withdrawal is KES 100.

🤝 Marketplace — Mashamba na Nyumba

A proximity-based marketplace connecting clients with nearby verified service providers. Contact views and listings cost one token unit (KES 2).

🏬 Wholesale — Wholesale Finder

A B2B marketplace connecting wholesale buyers with nearby suppliers. Customers order through the platform, pay via M-Pesa on delivery, and receive a receipt.

🛍️ E-commerce — Cashier Finder

An order platform connecting customers with local cashiers. Customers order from a shared catalogue, pay the cashier directly via M-Pesa on delivery, and receive a PDF receipt with QR verification.

🛵 SACCO — Bodaboda Badge System

Badge management for Busia County Bodaboda SACCO. Member registration, consent, and badge issuance.

About the suite

Five applications, all live, all hosted on Render with legal documentation, contact details, and data-handling policies published on each app. Built and maintained in Nairobi, Kenya.
