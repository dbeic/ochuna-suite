About Ochuna Suite

Ochuna Software Suite is an independent software initiative based in Kenya, focused on building practical digital tools for local businesses, farmers, community groups, and entrepreneurs across East Africa.

What we make

  • Harambee Cash App — a 30-second round game with a KES 10 entry and KES 200 welcome loan.
  • Mashamba na Nyumba — a proximity-based marketplace for services with KES 2 token units.
  • Wholesale Finder — a B2B marketplace connecting buyers with nearby wholesalers.
  • Cashier Finder — an order platform connecting customers with local cashiers.
  • Bodaboda Badge System — badge management for Busia County Bodaboda SACCO.

How we operate

  • We build in Kenya, for Kenya.
  • We use open web technologies — Python and Flask, PostgreSQL for structured data, and AWS S3 for uploaded images where a product needs image storage.
  • Every product publishes its own legal documents and contact details in the app itself.
  • We do not run advertising on the products, we do not sell user data, and we do not share personal information with third parties except for the service providers a product actually uses (for example, AWS S3 for file storage).

Current stage

All five applications are live and reachable from the Apps page. Each has its own terms, privacy policy, and user documentation published within the app.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, reliable, and locally-relevant software that solves real problems for Kenyan businesses and communities.
